Friend mode (family phone)

Installed web app on each relative’s phone. Messages arrive as a push notification with sound, and play as the picture strip and the spoken sentence.

One-tap picture replies go back to the tablet as a picture and a voice. “Wait” shows a 12-hour clock with the waiting time shaded.

The context line (last grapes, next meal) helps the adult answer consistently with the rest of the family.

The status toggle feeds the availability ring on Jonatito’s tablet. The calendar opens the shared Google Calendar.

🎙️ For Jonatito records a voice note. It shows up as a sound-wave badge on his tablet and stays on his voice shelf, so he can replay a familiar voice any time.

📍 Where I am sets the pin he sees on the globe (city only, optional “back on” date).

If a relative has no push notifications, the same message goes out on WhatsApp with a link.
